# GrokGrid motion ads

A 40 second launch ad for grokgrid.fun plus six ~22 second feature videos (live desk, calls, stats, matrix, launch a
bot, fee loop + burn), each in two cuts: 16:9 (1920x1080) and 9:16 (1080x1920), each with its own song.

Each video is a cut: `comp/cuts/<name>.js` (scenes) and `comp/tracks/<name>.js` (music), on the shared kit in
`comp/core.js` and `comp/audio-core.js`. Every video fades to black at the end (`comp/end.js`).

Everything is built by code, nothing is generated by a video model:

- The characters are the site's own flat bot marks (BotMark geometry), animated in code in `comp/core.js`: he hops in,
  trades on a coded phone and wins, the family lands in a lineup or runs across. No 3D, no rendered clips.
- `scripts/snapshot.js` saves the live site's real markup and stylesheets (desktop and mobile layouts), and
  `comp/ui.js` rebuilds them as live DOM inside the ad. No screenshots: every card, number, chart and button is
  the site's own code, so it stays sharp at any zoom and is animated piece by piece (the calls panel slides in,
  the wallet counts up, the chart draws, the cursor picks a bot colour and types a name with real hover states).
- `comp/` is the motion design: kinetic type, a camera that moves over the coded UI, trade chips, the 80/20 fee
  split, the wall of bots and the end card. `seek(t)` draws any frame.
- `comp/audio.js` writes and renders the soundtrack offline: a modern phone-ad style beat at 96 bpm (hard kick and snare, 16th hats with rolls, a gliding 808,
  bright keys and a chopped vocal-style hook) whose bars land on the cuts, plus pops, clicks, whooshes
  and a win chime timed to the picture.
- `scripts/render.js` steps the composition frame by frame in Chromium at 2x, and ffmpeg scales it down to full HD.

## Build

```
cd ad && ./make-ads.sh
```

Needs Node and ffmpeg. Output lands in `ad/out/`.
`WIDE_ONLY=1` renders only the 16:9 cuts; `CUTS="main calls"` renders only some videos.

Scene timing lives in `comp/comp.js` (one `scene(start, end, ...)` block per shot) and the matching sound cues in
`comp/audio.js` (`sfx()`).
