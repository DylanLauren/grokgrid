# GrokGrid motion ads

A 40 second launch ad for grokgrid.fun in two cuts: 16:9 (1920x1080) and 9:16 (1080x1920).

Everything is built by code, nothing is generated by a video model:

- `scripts/chars.py` builds Grok Bot and the colour family in Blender (same shapes, colours and pill eyes as the
  site's marks) and renders four transparent character clips: `hero` (hops in), `phone` (trades on his phone,
  the screen is the real site, then celebrates a win), `family_run` and `lineup` (end card).
- `scripts/capture.js` screenshots the live site (desktop and mobile) so every UI shot is the real product.
- `comp/` is the motion design: kinetic type, the real UI in browser and phone frames, a cursor that clicks
  through it, trade chips, the 80/20 fee split, the wall of bots and the end card. `seek(t)` draws any frame.
- `comp/audio.js` renders the sound offline: pops, clicks, whooshes and a win chime timed to the picture. There is
  no music bed, so a trending sound can go on top when posting.
- `scripts/render.js` steps the composition frame by frame in Chromium; ffmpeg encodes.

## Build

```
cd ad && ./make-ads.sh
```

Needs Blender (default `/Applications/Blender.app`), Node and ffmpeg. Output lands in `ad/out/`.
`SAMPLES=16 RES=540 ./make-ads.sh` makes a quick draft; `SKIP_CLIPS=1` reuses rendered 3D clips.

Scene timing lives in `comp/comp.js` (one `scene(start, end, ...)` block per shot) and the matching sound cues in
`comp/audio.js` (`sfx()`).
